# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First public release.

- Creature inspection: crouch to see resistances, weaknesses, immunities and modifiers under the
  nameplate; press `I` for the full explanation in your chat.
- 24 combat modifiers adapted from CreatureManager (offense, defense, affliction, special), at most
  one per group, shown as icons after the stars and as words in the creature name.
- Per-star body colours from 3 stars (StarLevelSystem palette) and a star counter above 5 stars.
- French and English texts, following the game language or forced in the config.
- Version check and server-enforced settings through ServerSync, as in CLLC: a player without the
  mod or with another version is refused by the server.
- Config in numbered sections, editable in game by admins with Ultimate Server Configuration Manager;
  every modifier's strength is configurable in "6 - Modifier values".
